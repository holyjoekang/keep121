The Problem

A chat app is only safe
while you can reach someone.

The family group chat is real-time. But it can't tell you where the one person who left the group is. On a trip to Yunnan, China with our parents, an elderly traveler mistook another bus for ours at a rest stop, and everyone's day stopped while the guide went looking.

26people's day came to a halt

One person left behind, about an hour of searching and waiting

Families and fellow travelers waited anxiously, and at the next stop the guide counted heads one by one again. Even on premium tours for parents, the "Count off!" roll call on the bus keeps repeating.

"In an age when you can see exactly where your taxi is, how do we check on the safety of the parents traveling with us?"— Where Keep121 began

Delays

While one person is searched for, the whole group stops. Searching and waiting are a cost.

Safety gaps

Departures are noticed late. On cable cars and in mountains, nobody even knows the location.

Guide workload

Roll call at every stop. Service quality drops while fatigue rises.

Unreachable

Lost elderly travelers often miss calls, and children have no phone. The answer isn't there when it matters most.

Photos and messages fly in real time, yet no one knows where the person who drifted away is.

See the solution
The Solution

Not a product where you pick devices,
an operations system where the itinerary sets the mode.

Travel agencies and schools only pick a product type and an itinerary. When the itinerary marks a "risky stretch" or a tag drifts away from the guide, the tag switches mode on its own. "Should we rent Keep Tag, Keep Tag+ or Shelter?" is no longer a question the agency has to answer.

Travelers

Wear it, and press one button when needed

All they do is consent before departure, wear the tag, and hold the button for 3 seconds if they get lost or hurt. No app, no login, no charging.

  • After SOS, a vibration says "the guide is coming"
  • Vibration alert 10 min before meeting time
Guides · Tour leaders

Fewer things to press, more things you can find

One-touch roll call, and if something happens, "who, where and how long ago" on a single alert card.

  • Group waiting time 98 → 38 min a day
  • 3-level alerts · bundled notifications
Families in Korea

"Confirmed" over coordinates

Which scene they're in now, and when the guide last counted heads. Incident alerts go out only after a person has confirmed.

  • App-free peace-of-mind link · KakaoTalk notices
  • Local and Korean time shown together
Agencies · Schools · Organizations

Upload the roster once, see results in reports

A product preset sets device mix, geofences and alert settings in one go. Keep121 handles kit delivery, charging and collection.

  • Billed per preset
  • Waiting and response time reports
Guide-as-Hub

The guide's phone becomes the hub,
and the itinerary sets the mode.

Tags signal the guide's phone (the hub) over BLE, and only a Keep Tag+ that drifts away from the hub reports its location directly over LTE. In the cloud, the itinerary and geofence engine builds a fence for each scene, and the alert engine filters events into 3 levels before passing them to guides, families and agencies.

How It Works

Instead of "1, 2, 3…" roll call,
one button.

From registration before departure to records after the trip, the guide's day comes down to four steps.

STEP 01

Upload roster · choose preset

Upload the roster and pick a product preset, and tag mapping for Nos. 1–26 and per-scene geofences are ready automatically.

STEP 02

One-tap roll call

A 5 m, 10 m or 20 m radius around the guide's phone. See instantly who is in and who is missing.

STEP 03

3-level departure alerts

After 180 seconds away from the hub, tracking starts and alerts are split into Urgent, Caution and Info. See the last location on the map right away.

STEP 04

Family sharing · trip journal

A peace-of-mind link for consenting family members, and after the trip, a memory page with routes and spots.
