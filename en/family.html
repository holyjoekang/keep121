For Families

The one line of confirmation
a chat app can't give.

What families need isn't live coordinates but "roughly where, and the guide has checked." They see it right away from a link received on KakaoTalk, with no app to install.

1
Scene name + last confirmation time

"Jade Dragon Snow Mountain cable car · head count 26/26 at 10:42." Local time and Korean time are shown together.

2
Only three notifications

Departure/arrival (airport · hotel), a daily summary (local evening), and when contact is needed (after the agency confirms).

3
Incident alerts only after a person confirms

If false alarms reach families as-is, trust collapses. In an emergency, the agency's staff confirm first, then notify by phone and KakaoTalk.

4
Only what was agreed, up to 3 people

The traveler chooses the sharing level (spot-level / exact location) and which family members receive it. The link closes 24 hours after return and becomes a trip album.

Location only during the trip, for safety, within the agreed scope

No surveillance

On corporate trips, the company can't see individual movements — only safety events. Tags switch off outside event hours.

Student autonomy

During free time, parents only see "inside the zone." Exact location opens only to the homeroom teacher and in emergencies.

Switch off anytime

Ask the guide to turn off family sharing. Attendance and urgent alerts stay on for group safety.

Deleted after 30 days

Raw location data is deleted 30 days after the trip ends; reports keep only aggregates without individual routes.

※ Korea's Location Information Act (consent for personal location data, business registration), guardian consent for under-14s and cross-border transfer of personal data are designed from general understanding and require legal review before launch.
