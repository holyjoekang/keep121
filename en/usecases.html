Shelter Scenario

Even on a cable car at 4,506 m,
you know who is where.

At mountain sights, people can fall or collapse from altitude sickness. Instead of assuming everything goes normally, you see in real time who is at which spot when it matters.

  1. 10:40
    Head count before boarding the cable car

    Mountain itinerary, so today the Keep Tag+ bands switch to Shelter mode. One-tap check 26/26 at the lower station, then boarding.

  2. 11:15
    Arrival check at the upper observatory

    The guide logs arrival at the spot. Families get an "Arrived at Jade Dragon Snow Mountain" notice.

  3. 11:52
    Anomaly alert

    No. 14 has been apart from the group and motionless for 15 minutes. The Keep121 Guide app shows the alert and last location.

  4. 11:58
    Go straight there by map and respond

    Arrived in 6 minutes following the map. With on-site staff, gave first aid to a traveler resting with altitude sickness.

  5. 12:30
    Descent · records saved

    26/26 confirmed for the cable car down. Alert and response history saved in the tour safety report.

※ A fictional scenario to explain the service. Some features, such as stop detection, are development and validation goals.

Use Cases

Starting with travel,
to anywhere people move together.

Our first target is the group travel industry. Once proven in the field, it expands into a core solution for "checking many people's safety at once."

First target

Senior · package tours

Multi-day trips abroad. Time to locate a fall on a snow mountain went from 65 minutes to 2, and risk exposure fell 80% (simulation). Our core revenue product.

Default Keep Tag+, Shelter only on cable car · snow mountain stretches

Day tours

Market and crowded-area fences plus a collection fence at the drop-off point. Keep Tag cut risk exposure by only 3%, while Keep Tag+ cut it by 39–57%.

Default Keep Tag+ for all · vehicle fence

School trips · field trips

Each class's hub is the homeroom teacher's phone. Time to locate a student who wandered off fell from about 90 minutes to 12. Parents see only "inside the zone."

Keep Tag+ with free time, Keep Tag without

Public events · large scale

Fixed hubs at checkpoints and off-course detection. For a 200-person walking event, Keep Tag+ cut risk by 81%, and Shelter for high-risk participants by 96%.

Keep Tag+ for all + Shelter for high-risk participants

Mountains · trekking · corporate hikes

Two hubs, at the front and rear, detect stragglers. Guided hikes saw 74% less risk with Keep Tag alone; trekking reached 94% with stretch Shelter.

Trekking Keep Tag+ + stretch Shelter · corporate hikes Keep Tag

Families · kids

Two parents' phones act as hubs. If a child drifts away, they're alerted within 60 seconds. In the theme park scenario, Keep Tag+ cut risk 92% and Keep Tag 50%.

Keep Tag+ · 60 s hub-exit preset

Jeju Special

Jeju: the whole island
as one safe zone.

Pick up a tag on arrival at the airport, travel with peace of mind, and return it when you leave. An idea talked about since beacons first appeared, now made real with low-power BLE plus GPS and LTE.

  1. Arrival · pick-up

    Pick up a tag at the airport info desk or car rental office, and link it to a guardian's phone by QR.

  2. Peace of mind on the go

    Keep Tag for city and sights, kept light. Instant alert if a child or companion drifts away.

  3. Risky-stretch mode switch

    On beaches, mid-mountain areas and Olle trails, it switches to Keep Tag+ Shelter or Seek mode.

  4. Return

    Collected at airport drop boxes, hotels and car rentals, then charged, sanitized and redistributed.
