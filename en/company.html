Every one, kept.

One door, one dot.

The Keep121 mark is a door made from the numbers 1·2·1. The two 1s are the pillars, and the 2 becomes the arch over them. The single dot inside the door is "that one person" we keep to the end. The promise to guard the way out and the way back, from departure to return, drawn in a single picture.

1 · 2 · 1 — the one who leaves, all of us keeping watch, the one who returns. Keep every one, from going out to coming in. Keep121 · the promise in our name

Count every one

Call people by name

Treat people as people, not numbers, and ask for their consent first.

Guard only the going and coming

Locations are collected only during the trip, and the link expires on return.

Go after the one who's lost

Seek mode and 3-level alerts reach the one who drifted away.
