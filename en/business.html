For Travel Agencies

HQ control that doesn't wait
for the guide's report.

Most field guides belong to local land operators or are contracted per tour. When something happens, they try to handle it first, and the report to HQ easily slips. Meanwhile a small separation can turn into a serious incident and a dispute. Keep121 sends alerts from the tag-linked guide app to HQ at the same time.

Today
  1. Separation or incident occurs
  2. The guide handles it alone
  3. HQ learns late — from a report after the fact or a family complaint
  4. What happened relies on memory and chat logs
Keep121
  1. The tag detects separation or SOS automatically
  2. Alerts reach the guide app and the HQ control screen at once
  3. If the guide doesn't acknowledge within a set time, the HQ staff on duty is called automatically
  4. Times of incident → awareness → report → action are logged on a timeline automatically

How it's used differently by agency size

Large travel agencies

Hundreds of departures from one operations room

  • Unified control of concurrent tours from the HQ operations room
  • Rate land operators and guides on data, not gut feeling
  • Objective records for media and dispute response after an incident
  • Integration (API) that takes rosters straight from the booking system

Sign-off point · brand risk and crisis response

Small and mid-sized agencies

The owner's phone is the operations room

  • Check every tour on mobile, without a separate operations room
  • No equipment to buy — pay only for tours you run
  • Stand out from large agencies with "senior tours where no one is missed"
  • Keep response records so one incident doesn't decide the company's survival

Sign-off point · product trust with a small team

It isn't a tool for watching guides, but a record that proves the guide responded on time. Locations are collected only from consenting travelers during the trip, and HQ control is planned from stage 2 of the B2B roadmap.
