Where It Began · Lijiang, Yunnan
"In an age when you can see exactly where your taxi is,
how do we check on the safety
of the parents traveling with us?"

On a trip to Yunnan with our parents, an elderly traveler boarded the wrong bus at a rest stop and 26 people's day came to a halt. Keep121 began in that one hour.
